# yt-autopilot · 9:16 voiceover script

For `brag.mp4` and `brag-vo-bed.mp4` (22 s, vertical). About 45 words, which is roughly 14 s of speech, so there is room to breathe.

**Delivery:** calm, dry, a little pleased with itself. Don't announce it, just say it.
**Say the name as:** "yt-autopilot" → "why-tee autopilot".

## Read-through

> What if uploading was just a sentence?
>
> Meet yt-autopilot.
>
> Claude drafts the title and hashtags, uploads it private. Then you say, "publish it." And it's live.
>
> It checks Shorts hashtag rules first. Plus a weekly report.
>
> Upload by name. Publish when you say so.

**Silent on purpose:** 0.00–0.40 (the first hit lands on its own), 4.90–6.45 (the chat window opens and the viewer reads the message), 12.20–14.20 (let "Published" sit).

## If a line runs long

- Drop the connector words: "And", "Then", "Plus". Every line still works without them (line 5 becomes "You say, publish it.").
- Alternate hook, if you'd rather mirror the headline: "Your upload button? It's a sentence." (fits the same 0.40–2.70 window).

## Recording and mixing

- Use **`brag-vo-bed.mp4`**: the music is already ducked to voice level and the sound effects are about 2.5 dB lower. Put your voice on its own track above it and leave the bed alone.
- **`brag.mp4`** is the same video at the normal music level, for use without a voiceover.
- Don't talk past 21.9 s; the video ends at 22.0.
- Each line matches something the project does (see "Claims and where they come from" in `brag-plan.md`), so you can say it without caveats.
